# Business Problem


Global Superstore is a multinational retail company that wants to understand its sales performance across different markets, products, and customer segments.

The objective of this project is to analyze historical sales data and answer key business questions such as:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which categories are the most profitable?
- Which countries and markets contribute the most sales?
- Who are the top 10 customers?
- How do discounts affect profitability?
- Which shipping modes incur the highest costs?

The analysis aims to help business stakeholders make data-driven decisions to improve profitability, optimize inventory, and identify growth opportunities.
